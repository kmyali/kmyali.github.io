Projects and Hackathons
  • U-Calendar
    • U-Calendar is a desktop calendar application specifically designed to help university students manage various deadlines and track their grades.
    • The application includes weekly/monthly views, adding/changing classes, and a grade calculator.
    • Technologies used include Java, JavaFX, SQLite, CSS, Maven, UML, Git/GitHub.
    • Credits: this was a group project during "ECE 5010: Software Design" in Winter 2021 with my classmates Mohamed Shahwan and Ayman Abu-Awad. Link to the GitHub repository.
  • TEK Basketball: a project at MakeUofT2020
    • At my first-ever hardware hackathon, I teamed up with two energetic students (whom I had just met) at Univeristy of Toronto, and we decided to do something new.
    • Using Arduino, Ultrasonic sensors, an LCD screen, and some cut-out cardboard, we developed a simple basketball game as a tribute to Kobe Bryant.
    • Remarks: Ultrasonic sensors were inconsistent in detecting the ball, but the overall experiene was quite fun. Link to devpost.
  • IEEE Xtreme Programming 2019
    • In my first-ever coding competition, I participated in the IEEE Xtreme 13.0 Programming challenge at MUN.
    • After 24 hours of straight coding with little rest where my team endured numerous frustrations trying to get the code to work, we ended up ranking in the top 33% worldwide out of 4000 teams.
    • This competition opened my eyes to the challenges that developers face on a daily basis and made me realize the importance of collaboration in software.
    • Link to my LinkedIn post where I spoke a bit about this experience.
Extracurriculars
  • Paradigm Hyperloop
    SpaceX Hyperloop Competition, Los Angelos, July 2019. I can be seen peeking in the top right.
    • Paradigm Hyperloop is a student team that designs and builds a Hyperloop Pod for the SpaceX Hyperloop Competition.
    • As a member on the Hyperloop team from October 2018 to October 2020, this experience opened my eyes to the design process of (relatively) large-scale engineering projects and the work that goes behind them.

  • Hack Frost NL
    A photo of some team members during a team social in August 2021
    • Hack Frost NL is a student initiative that started in October 2020 out of my desire to start Memorial University's first large-scale hackathon.
    • As the lead organizer for Hack Frost NL, I am responsible for planning the overall team strategy, ensuring that the team is on-track, and that everyone is getting something out of the events whether it's the students or the organizers.
    • The first hackathon we did attracted more than 130 bright students, 10 sponsors, and 5 partners. After the hackathon's impact, we decided to expand and are now doing year-round events such as networking events, bootcamps, and interviews with local tech entrepreneurs.
    • Keep an eye out for Hack Frost NL 2.0!
