Hello 👋! My name is Kamal Ali

I am a 4th year Computer Engineering student at Memorial Univeristy of Newfoundland class of 2023.

I am also the founder and lead organizer for Hack Frost NL, a student initiative that aims to bridge the gap between the classroom and the workplace by organizing hands-on workshops and an annual hackathon for free.

Projects and Hackathons
  • U-Calendar
    U-Calendat is a desktop calendar application specifically designed to help university students manage various deadlines and track their grades. The application was developed as part of the Software Design course (ECE 5010) in Winter 2021. The application includes weekly/monthly views, adding/changing classes, and a grade calculator. Technologies used: Java, JavaFX, SQLite, CSS, Maven, UML, Git/GitHub. Credits: this was a group project with the help of Mohamed Shahwan and Ayman Abu-Awad. Repository
  • Tek Basketball: a project at MakeUofT2020
    At my first-ever hardware hackathon in Toronto, I teamed up with. Technologies used include Arduino, Ultrasonic sensors, and an LCD screen for display of score. Remarks: Ultrasonic sensors were inconsistent in detecting the ball, but the overall experiene was quite fun. Link to devpost.

Extracurriculars
  • Paradigm Hyperloop
    What's Paradigm? Paradigm Hyperloop is a student team that designs and builds a Hyperloop Pod for the SpaceX Hyperloop Competition. What I did there? I was part of the Hyperloop team from October 2018 to October 2020. This team opened my eyes to the design process of (relatively) large-scale engineering projects and the work that goes behind them. Teamwork, dedicaiton, passion. In my first year, I was involved with the electrical team and. I got the chance to go to SpaceX to participate in the competition. In my second year, I took part with the software team. I developed scripts using Python, learned about collaboration using Git, and tried my hands with APIs.
  • Hack Frost NL
    Hack Frost NL is a student initiative that started in October 2020 out of the desire to start Memorial University's first large-scale hackathon. As the lead organizer for Hack Frost NL, I am responsible for planning the team's efforts, ensuring that the team is on-track, and that everyone is getting something out of the events whether it's the students or the organizers. The first hackathon we did attracted more than 130 bright students, 10 sponsors, and 5 partners. Through the projects students worked on for 48 hours, we helped students apply their problem-solving skills to solve a real-world problem. Watch out for Hack Frost NL 2.0!
